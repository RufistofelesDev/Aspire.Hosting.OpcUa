## Read this before you take the free option

This is a **library**, not a server, and that changes what the AGPL asks of you.

RufistoWard is something you deploy. Its AGPL obligation only reaches you if you
*modify* it and then offer it to others over a network. Most people who run it owe
nothing.

This package is different. You add it with `<PackageReference>` and call it from your
own AppHost, which makes your AppHost a work that combines with AGPL code. Under the
free option, **your project inherits the copyleft obligation** — if you distribute it
or offer it over a network, your users are entitled to your source.

In practice:

- **Building something open source, or a demo, or an internal tool you never
  distribute?** The AGPL costs you nothing. Take it and go.
- **Referencing this from a closed-source commercial product?** You need the commercial
  licence. Email me — this is a small package and I am not precious about it.
